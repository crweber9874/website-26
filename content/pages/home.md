## hero

eyebrow: The Arizona Voter Project · Web Modules
title: Data tools and analysis to demystify Arizona politics

The Arizona Voter Project is a non-partisan initiative supported by the University of Arizona&rsquo;s School of Government and Public Policy. It provides tools and analysis to better capture the political, social, and cultural richness of the Arizona electorate — developing resources to access, experiment, and interact with data so everyone can learn more about politics and communities within the state.

Since 2021, the project has archived the state&rsquo;s voter file and joined it with U.S. Census and other federal datasets. In collaboration with the Arizona Policy Lab at the University of Arizona, it also fields the <em>Arizona Voter Survey</em> — a scientific survey of likely voters, with a sample constructed to mirror the population that participates in Arizona elections. Every section here is built on disclosure-safe aggregates; no individual record is ever published.
