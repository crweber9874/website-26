## hero

eyebrow: Undergraduate course
title: Democracy in Data: Exploring Arizona&rsquo;s Political Landscape

An eight-week seminar in political science and civic data literacy. Students learn to read Arizona&rsquo;s voter file and related public aggregates through interactive modules on this site — maps, survey themes, transition flows, and election results — without turning the classroom into a statistics course.

## intro

Each week below includes learning objectives, suggested readings and activities, and links to the AVP modules that ground discussion. Canonical copies of week outlines also live in the repository&rsquo;s <code>pedagogy/</code> directory for instructors who prefer Markdown in Git.

This course sidesteps formulas and emphasizes visualization, comparison, and plain-language reasoning — the same habits journalists, advocates, and campaign professionals use when they ask whether a number answers the question they actually care about.
