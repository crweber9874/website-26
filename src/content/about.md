I am a Professor in the School of Government and Public Policy at the
University of Arizona, where I direct
[The Arizona Voter Project](https://voterproject.arizona.edu). I received my
Ph.D. in political science from Stony Brook University in 2008.

My research sits at the intersection of political psychology and political
behavior, with a focus on American political campaigns, ideology, and
authoritarianism. I also work extensively in political methodology ---
experimental design, measurement, and, increasingly, the data science of large
voter files. My work has appeared in the *American Journal of Political
Science*, the *Journal of Politics*, *Political Behavior*, *Political
Psychology*, and elsewhere.
