The Arizona Voter Project assembles and documents Arizona's statewide voter
file across build dates, then turns it into research infrastructure and public
data products. I direct the project with support from a University of Arizona
RII grant.

The [public modules](/voter) are in beta: figures are preliminary and will
migrate to the official project site.

- **[My Story](/voter/story)** — a guided walk through what a voter file is and
  what it records about a registrant.
- **[Explorer](/voter/explorer)** — registration, roll churn, and time-series
  comparisons across counties, districts, precincts, and block groups.
- **[Party](/voter/party)** — party transitions estimated from a
  continuous-time Markov model, so irregular file windows share one rate clock.
- **[Common Ground](/voter/agreement)** — where Arizonans agree, by geography
  and party.
- **[Voting](/voter/voting)** — precinct-level turnout and vote distributions.
- **[Understanding the file](/voter/understanding)** — field definitions,
  de-identification, small-cell suppression, and build-over-build comparisons.
